About Cameron Harding

Bringing Fashion & Commercial Briefs To Life

"I'm a 26 year old professional model based in Yorkshire, with over two years of experience spanning runway and commercial work."

Fashion has always been more than clothing to me. It's a form of art, and every shoot is a chance to help bring a piece of fashion to life through imagery. That passion is what drives everything I do in front of the camera.

Whatever the brief, I will bring energy, professionalism and a genuine love for the craft. I welcome all enquiries, so get in touch and let's create something great together.

01. Versatility

Seamless transition between high-fashion editorial runway, commercial lifestyle, and athletic campaigns.

02. Professionalism

High work ethic, punctual, adaptable on-set energy with clear direction-following skills.

03. Physicality

Maintained athletic 42" chest / 34" waist build with high camera presence and natural poise.
